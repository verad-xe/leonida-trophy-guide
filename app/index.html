About & sources

How this guide was made

Every trophy entry starts from the official PlayStation 5 list: the name, description and grade are reproduced exactly, in the official order. The guide text, steps, kind, roadmap stage and missable assessment for each trophy are original editorial work written for this guide, grounded in what the official description actually says. Where a description is vague by design, the guide says so, offers the most reasonable reading of its wording, and labels that reading as an inference rather than presenting it as fact.

What is official, what is editorial

Official: trophy names, descriptions, grades and the total of 55. Editorial: all guidance, the six-stage roadmap, difficulty and time estimates, missable flags and strategy notes. Estimates are labelled as estimates wherever they appear. The difficulty framing draws on trophy-design precedent from earlier open-world games in the same tradition, named below, and is not presented as information about unreleased game systems.
