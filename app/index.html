About & sources

How this guide was made

The tracker data — every trophy's name, description and grade — is the official PlayStation 5 list for Grand Theft Auto VI, all 55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze. Everything else is editorial: the per-trophy guides are original writing created for this app, the roadmap is an ordering built from the official descriptions, and the missable flags are reasoned judgements from what those descriptions say, each with its reason shown.

What is official vs editorial: names, descriptions and grades are official. Guides, steps, kinds, stages and missable flags are editorial. Difficulty (4/10) and time (80–120 hours) are labelled estimates based on series precedent, not measurements. Interpretations of cryptic descriptions — Challenging Tank, Personal Best, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Actions Speak Louder — are labelled as inferences inside their guides.

Sources, named

  • PlayStation official trophy list for Grand Theft Auto VI (PlayStation 5, 2026-11-18) — the source of every name, description and grade, and of the tracked counts cited in guides.
  • Grand Theft Auto V trophy design and 100% checklist structure (Rockstar Games, 2013) — precedent for the roadmap structure, strand structure and completion planning.
  • Red Dead Redemption 2 trophy design and set collecting (Rockstar Games, 2018) — precedent for the difficulty estimate and set-based collecting method.
  • The competition starter data for this guide, which carried the official list and its counts (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 Top Fun locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas of Leonida, 9 gangs, 8 garages, 500 tracked choices).

Sources are cited by name beside each trophy's guide. This app contains no links and loads nothing: a citation here is a name you can look up yourself, not a request the app makes.

Offline & privacy

The guide works fully offline and makes no network requests. Your progress is stored only in this browser's local storage, never leaves your device, and can be exported to a JSON file, imported back, or reset at any time from the header panel.

No Rockstar Games logos, artwork, box art or game screenshots appear anywhere in this app. All design is original CSS with system fonts.
