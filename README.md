Leonida Legend is a complete offline trophy guide and tracker for the 55 official PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. It combines a searchable, filterable 55-trophy tracker with an original guide, concrete steps and a cited source for every trophy, plus grade, kind and status filters, a missable-only view and spoiler protection that hides unearned story trophies by default. Progress is stored only in your browser, and can be exported, imported or reset at any time.

The guide also includes a six-stage platinum roadmap covering story, strands and side stories, the 100 percent push, collectibles and counters, money and property, and final cleanup, together with a dedicated missables strategy and honest, inference-labelled guidance for the cryptic hidden trophies. Difficulty and play-time figures are clearly labelled editorial estimates based on precedent, not official facts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. No build step and no network requests: everything the guide loads is under app/.
